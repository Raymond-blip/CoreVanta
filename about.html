Our Story

Founded in 2018, CoreVanta began with one simple vision — to make remote work accessible, reliable, and rewarding. We noticed the growing need for skilled professionals who could work efficiently from anywhere, and U.S. companies seeking trustworthy remote teams.

What started as a small team of passionate founders and 15 remote workers has grown into a thriving global community. We faced early challenges — building trust in remote work, establishing quality standards, and creating fair compensation structures. But we remained committed to our core belief: that talented professionals deserve legitimate opportunities regardless of their location.

Today, we're proud to serve as a bridge between talent and opportunity, helping businesses grow while empowering workers to build careers without boundaries. Our success is measured not just in revenue or client count, but in the thousands of lives we've positively impacted through stable, flexible employment.
