  2. Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you apply for a position or contact us through CoreVanta, we collect personal information including your full name, email address, phone number, country of residence, and resume or application materials. We only collect information that is necessary for recruitment and communication purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to review your application, contact you regarding open positions, and assess your suitability for remote roles at CoreVanta. We do not use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your personal data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized HR personnel. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

4. Sharing Your Information

CoreVanta does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data with our U.S. partner companies strictly for the purpose of evaluating your application, and only with your knowledge.

5. Data Retention

We retain your application data for up to 12 months from the date of submission. After this period, your data is securely deleted unless you have been hired or have provided consent for continued retention.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us via our Contact page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our Contact page.
